WELCOME TO THE SQUAD

This is EF49.

A gaming hub made for people who love discovering new worlds, competing with friends, and finding their next favorite game. Pick a game, explore a genre, and level up your play.

No accounts, no sign-ups: just a hand-picked library you can search and filter by genre, with every card linking straight to the game's store page. EF49 is a discovery catalog, so it never hosts or distributes the games.
